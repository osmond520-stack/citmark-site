Methodology

How CitMark measures visibility

We ask AI assistants the questions your buyers actually ask, and record whether your brand gets mentioned in the answers. Measured, not modeled.

What we measure

Organic visibility — whether AI assistants name your brand on their own in answers to real buying questions. Paid placements, including sponsored units shown on free plans, are outside the scope of measurement and are never counted in your score.

The questions

We use buyer-intent (category) questions — the kind a customer asks before they know your name: "best X for Y" lists, comparisons, scenario advice. We deliberately don't pad scores with branded questions ("tell me about [your brand]") — that's a vanity metric, not a buying moment.

The engines

Every full check runs your questions across 3 AI engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Pro adds DeepSeek (synthetic); Mistral joins with Teams, which isn't on sale yet. If one engine has a bad day, it never blocks the others.

Claude was included in our measurement until 7 November 2026 and is no longer measured.

The rhythm

A full measurement runs whenever you start a snapshot, so every number comes from a real run. Automatic re-checks aren't live yet.

What counts

A mention = your brand appears in the answer. Visibility score = answers mentioning your brand ÷ valid answers × 100. Failed or timed-out runs don't count toward the score — they're reported separately as an error rate, so you can always see data quality.

How the bands are computed. Every interval on this page is calculated from the answers actually collected for that run — a Wilson score interval at the run's real sample size, not a fixed constant. A wider band means fewer answers were collected; it is the uncertainty made visible, not a weaker method. Where a sample is too small to support a reading, we say so instead of showing a number.

Category baselines. Baselines are published per category only after that category accumulates enough brands and repeated runs to support one. Until then, no baseline is shown for that category.

The sources behind the answers

Mentions are only half the picture. When an AI engine returns sources with its answer — links or references it drew on — CitMark records them and shows them attributed to that engine and that question, so you can see which sources AI models cite when answering questions in your category, and whether your site is among them.

When an engine doesn't return sources for an answer, that answer is marked "sources not provided." We never reconstruct, infer, or fill in a source list the engine didn't give — a citation you see in CitMark is one the engine actually returned.

What you get

Every run: your score change, what drove it, and concrete actions — across three fronts: on-site (your own website), off-site (where the web talks about you: reviews, forums, press), and technical (the structured signals AI engines read). (The dashboard's there if you want it.)

What we don't do

We don't guarantee AI recommendations or rankings — no honest tool can. We measure credibly, explain plainly, and help you improve.

Step: Claim your business on ChatGPT first.

Before you read third-party numbers, claim and verify your business profile in ChatGPT's Business Hub. Verification can use a code by text, call or email, a DNS TXT record, or your Shopify sign-in. The blue check is a separate, second step: identity verification through Persona. Local businesses can claim a listing that already exists in the US.

Claiming doesn't change how visible you are in ChatGPT. It gives you your profile's own aggregate stats — mentions, site clicks and discovery topics over 7 or 28 days. Share of voice isn't part of the Hub beta, and Business Agents can't take payments or bookings yet.

FAQ

How do the checks actually work?

Every time you run a check, we send your question set to each engine and capture the answers. We extract brand mentions and the sources cited, aggregate citations by site, and compute your visibility score. If an engine fails or times out, that run is excluded from the score and flagged as an error — we never silently fill gaps. The same rule applies to sources: if an engine returns no sources for an answer, we mark them as not provided instead of guessing.

How can I see which sources AI models cite in my category?

Run a check. For every answer where the engine returns sources, we extract them, aggregate them by site, and show them next to the question and engine they came from. Where an engine returns no sources, the answer is marked "sources not provided" rather than left blank or estimated.

Which AI engines are covered?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini on every plan; Growth the same three, Pro adds DeepSeek (synthetic); Mistral joins with Teams, which isn't on sale yet. Every engine runs the same questions, so scores stay comparable across engines and over time.

Do I need to install anything or share analytics access?

No. There's nothing to install, and we don't need access to your analytics. If you later choose to connect Google Search Console, that's a separate, explicit authorization — always your call.
