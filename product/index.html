— How the score works

Measured, not modeled.

Your score is the share of valid answers that mention your brand, shown with a 95% Wilson band at the run’s real sample size. Failed answers don’t count, and a source is only shown when an engine actually returned it.

— The facts a draft may use

Your facts, nothing else.

Drafts are written only from the statements you confirm in Your facts. That’s why a correction drafted by CitMark never invents a claim about your business.
