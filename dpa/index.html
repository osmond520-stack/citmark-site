Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between you (the "Customer") and CitMark. It states, in processor terms, the same practices our Privacy Policy describes in plain language.

1. Roles and scope

You are the controller of the personal data your workspace submits to CitMark. CitMark is the processor. We process that data to provide the service described in the Terms and the Privacy Policy: running AI visibility measurements, producing reports, administering your account and billing, preventing fraud and abuse, and keeping the service secure.

2. Acting on your instructions

We process the personal data in your workspace only to provide the service, following your instructions as expressed through your use of CitMark, your workspace settings, and your support requests. We will not use it for any other purpose. If we ever believe an instruction would breach data-protection law, we will tell you; if a law requires us to process the data differently, we will tell you first unless the law forbids it.

3. Data and data subjects

  • Account data — email addresses, names (where provided), and workspace membership of you and your workspace members.
  • Brand & measurement setup — the brands, domains, and questions you ask us to track (business data).
  • Measurement data — AI answers we collect when running checks, extracted citations, scores, and reports.
  • Connection tokens — Google or Shopify OAuth tokens, only if you connect those services.
  • Usage & security logs — logins, API calls, and admin actions.

4. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit and at rest. OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM via a key management service before storage and never appear in logs.
  • Every data query is scoped to your workspace; cross-workspace access is denied by default.
  • All employee access to personal data is logged (who, when, what) and audited.
  • We request the minimum OAuth scopes needed, and each new scope is reviewed before it is added.
  • We never sell your data.

5. Sub-processors

We use the providers listed on our Sub-processors page, and only the providers strictly necessary to run CitMark. Each is bound by its own data-protection terms. That list is part of this DPA. We will give at least 30 days’ notice before a new sub-processor starts processing personal data, by updating the Sub-processors page and emailing workspace owners. If you object to a new sub-processor, you may terminate your subscription before the change takes effect. Where a change is urgently required for security or continuity, we may make it immediately and notify you promptly afterwards.

6. Where data lives and international transfers

Your account, brand, measurement data, tokens, and logs are stored in our primary database in Singapore (Supabase). The backend runs on Google Cloud in Tokyo, Japan. The website itself is served globally (GitHub Pages + Cloudflare edge network). Where the GDPR applies, transfers to our providers are protected by appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. If something goes wrong

If we discover a data breach, we will: contain it immediately (revoke tokens, block access); assess what was affected within 24 hours; notify affected users within 72 hours — and, where you are a business customer, notify you as controller within the same 72 hours, with the information you need for your own notifications — and notify regulators as required by GDPR; and publish a post-incident report within 7 days.

8. Rights, export, and deletion

You can download all your personal data as JSON from your account settings at any time. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data from our live systems within 30 days; encrypted backups rotate out within about 90 days and pending deletions are re-applied if a backup is ever restored. Anonymized measurement data may be kept for category benchmarks. For any rights request — access, correction, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent — email support@citmark.com; we respond within 30 days. Security issues: security@citmark.com.

9. Checking our compliance

On request, we will provide the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate that we meet this DPA — for example our security documentation and answers to a security questionnaire. Requests go to support@citmark.com.
