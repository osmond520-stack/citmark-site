How we check

No black box — here's exactly what we do

Anyone can show you a score. This is how ours is produced, so you can judge for yourself whether it's worth trusting.

WEEKLY · A DOZEN QUESTIONS

Every week we ask a dozen real buyer questions — the things your customers type into AI right before they buy — in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

4 ENGINES · EVERY ANSWER LOGGED

For every answer we record whether your business is mentioned, whether your pages are cited as sources, and which competitors appear instead of you.

LLMS.TXT · 5 CHECKS

We grade your llms.txt on five things: it exists and isn't empty, it has a main heading, it's organised into sections, it links your pages with descriptions, and it covers the essentials — products, docs, API, contact.
