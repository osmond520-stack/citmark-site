How we check

No black box — here's exactly what we do

Anyone can show you a score. This is how ours is produced, so you can judge for yourself whether it's worth trusting.

YOUR TRACKED QUESTIONS

We ask your tracked buyer questions — the things your customers type into AI right before they buy — in ChatGPT · Perplexity · Gemini.

UP TO 6 ENGINES · EVERY ANSWER LOGGED

What gets monitored

Everything that decides whether AI recommends you

01Brand mentions

Does the answer name your business at all? We track mention rate per engine, per question, run over run — the earliest warning sign when you start slipping.

02Citations & source links

Mentions are nice; citations send traffic. We record exactly which of your pages each engine links to as a source, and which competitor pages beat yours.

03Competitor share of voice

See your slice of AI answers next to up to five competitors — and get alerted when a rival starts winning questions that used to be yours.

Plus: llms.txt health monitoring, and a plain-English fix-it brief in every report — the actions, not just the scores.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

1. Who we are

CitMark ("we", "us") operates citmark.com. For any privacy question or request: support@citmark.com.

2. What we collect

Account data: your email, name (if you provide one), and workspace membership. Brand & measurement setup: the brands, domains, and questions you ask us to track. Measurement data: AI answers we collect when running checks, extracted citations, scores, and reports. Connection tokens (only if you connect Google or Shopify): stored encrypted — never in plain text, never in logs. Usage & security logs: logins, API calls, admin actions. What we never collect: payment card details — payments go through Creem, our merchant of record, and card data never touches our systems.

3. Why we use it, and the lawful basis

  • To run the service — measurements, reports, and alerts (performance of the contract).
  • To administer your account and billing — contract.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and keep the service secure — legitimate interest.
  • To improve the product, using anonymized, aggregated data only — legitimate interest.
  • Where the GDPR applies, the lawful bases above refer to GDPR Art. 6.

4. How we store and protect it

  • Your data is stored in our primary database in Singapore, encrypted in transit and at rest. OAuth tokens are encrypted before storage and never appear in logs.
  • We never sell your data. We share it with no third party except the service providers strictly necessary to run CitMark (hosting, email delivery), each bound by their own data-protection terms.

5. International transfers

Your data lives in Singapore — your account, brand, measurement data, tokens, and logs are stored in our primary database there. The website itself is served globally. Some providers handle specific tasks elsewhere, and only the data they need for that task: email delivery — Resend (United States); payment processing — Creem (United States). Where the GDPR applies, transfers to these providers are protected by appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

Account and workspace data: until you delete your account. Billing records: as long as tax law requires. Usage logs: 90 days; security audit logs: 1 year.

7. Your rights

You may at any time: access, correct, or delete your data; withdraw your consent; restrict or object to processing; request portability; and (in the EU/UK) lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Email support@citmark.com for any of these — we respond within 30 days. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out.

8. Children

This page is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will post any updates here with a new effective date. Material changes get an email notice before they take effect.
