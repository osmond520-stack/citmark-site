## Where Otterly genuinely wins

### More engines, including the big three surfaces

Otterly tracks seven AI engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini and Claude. CitMark sells three today — four on Pro, adding DeepSeek.

If AI Overviews, AI Mode or Copilot coverage decides it, choose Otterly.

### More prompts at the mid tier

Standard (€189/mo) includes 120 prompts — 15,000 credits a month. Our question sets are smaller by design and counted in points, not raw prompts.

If you track hundreds of prompts every day, their mid tier carries more raw volume.

### A wide free-tool and audit surface

A GEO Audit plus 14 free tools on a dedicated subdomain. Our free surface is one snapshot.

If you want free audits before paying anything, they offer more doors in.

### An agency form

Pitch Workspaces, white-label reporting and unified billing, built for agencies. CitMark has no agency workspace today.

Agencies pitching clients will find a shape built for them.

### API and MCP access

Standard includes 2,000 API and 2,000 MCP requests a month. CitMark does not offer API access today.

If your workflow pipes this data into other tools, that access is real.

If those are your deciding rows, buy Otterly — sincerely.

## The difference

Otterly stops at the dashboard: scores, audits, gap lists. What lands on your desk is a list of problems, and the fixing is still yours. CitMark runs the full loop — Measure. Diagnose. Draft. Morning: the dashboard shows your visibility across seven engines. A rival gained citations on two prompts; the content-gap view lists pages to write.

Afternoon: you open a document and start drafting the fix yourself.

What you hold at 6 pm: a clearer list of problems.

On our side the same daily run uses 210 question sets a week: Growth's pool of 150 covers five runs a week, not daily; Pro's 400 covers it.

120 prompts · 15,000 credits/mo · 4 of 7 engines included. This workload uses 3,600 of those credits (30 prompts × 4 engines × 30 days).

### CitMark Pro — $129/mo

400 question sets per rolling 7 days · 4 engines. This workload uses 210 a week. (Growth, $49, pools 150 a week — five runs, not daily.)

The units differ — prompts × engines × days in credits there, questions × runs against a weekly pool here; both are published in full on the pricing pages. At this workload the difference is €189 against $129, and what it buys is engine breadth (conceded above), not the fix.
