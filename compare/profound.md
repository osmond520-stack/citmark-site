## Where Profound genuinely wins

### Enterprise depth

SOC 2 compliance, SSO/SAML, dedicated support, and unlimited domains and seats on tailored packages. CitMark has no enterprise security layer today.

If your procurement requires SOC 2 and SSO on day one, that requirement decides it.

### More answer engines

Up to 10 answer engines, including Exa Search, Muse Spark and Qwen alongside the familiar names. CitMark sells three — four on Pro.

If you need the widest engine panel an enterprise contract can buy, theirs is wider.

### Agents that act

Profound's AI Marketer runs agents on credits — drafting and acting across your team's workflows. CitMark's drafts stop at your approval inbox; nothing acts for you.

If you want automation that goes beyond drafts, their agents go further.

If those are your deciding rows, buy Profound — sincerely.
