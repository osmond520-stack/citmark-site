CREDIT WHERE DUE

Where Scrunch genuinely wins

Eight platforms, on by default

Scrunch Core includes eight AI platforms by default — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Grok and Meta AI — with no choose-three step. We sell three; Pro makes four.

If platform coverage decides it, choose Scrunch.

Any country, any language — set per prompt

Both Scrunch tiers list countries, languages and personas as Any, and a prompt can be set for a specific country, with results filtered by country. We don't meter by country or language — and we don't offer per-country prompt setup either.

If you run prompts market by market, that setup is theirs.

Pages generated and optimized for you

Core includes 10 pages generated and 10 pages optimized a month; Enterprise adds Agent Pages. We generate no pages at all — drafts only, and nothing is ever published by us.

If you want the pages produced for you, that is their half of the job.

If those are your deciding rows, buy Scrunch — sincerely.

THE DIFFERENCE

They show you the problem. We draft the fix — and show our work.

Scrunch covers the wider surface — eight platforms on by default, prompts in any country, pages generated for you. That is one dimension, and it is theirs. The other dimension is what happens to a single answer: whether a wrong mention gets separated from a right one, whether the problem becomes a diagnosis, and whether the diagnosis becomes a draft. CitMark runs the full loop — Measure. Diagnose. Draft. The diagnosis becomes a draft, written from your confirmed facts, waiting in your approval inbox. You review, you send — CitMark never publishes or contacts a source for you.

What a day leaves you holding

A Tuesday with Scrunch

Afternoon: you open a document and start drafting the fix yourself.

Morning: a snapshot scores your brand. Win citations names the sources citing rivals instead. A mention of your brand is wrong — you mark it as a factual error.

What you hold at 6 pm: the fix, drafted and reviewed.

QUESTIONS

Why should I trust a comparison page written by CitMark?

Because the concessions are in the table, not just the text: every row where Scrunch wins is tagged Their win, every fact carries the check date on this page, and we re-check on any rival price or plan change we spot — and quarterly at the latest. Scrunch covers eight platforms and any country. Why would I choose CitMark?

If the job is the widest surface — eight platforms, any country, pages generated — yes, those rows are theirs, sincerely. If the job is what happens after the measurement — a wrong mention separated from a right one, a diagnosis, then a draft you can send — that is the half we built.

This page carries the date its facts were checked, at the top and under the table. We re-check on any rival pricing or plan change we spot, and quarterly at the latest; the date moves when the facts are re-verified, not before.
