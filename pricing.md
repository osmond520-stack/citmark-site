### How much should a small SaaS team budget for AI visibility tracking?

One plan is enough — you don't pay per seat, and every check draws from the same monthly points balance. Budget $25/mo at launch pricing ($35/mo standard) for Starter, or $49/mo ($70/mo standard) for Growth if you're tracking more brands and questions. ### Do you charge by credits or per check?

No credits, and nothing metered per tool. Every plan includes a monthly points balance — 260 points on Starter, 650 on Growth, 1,732 on Pro — and each check draws from that single balance. If you run low, point packs top up the same balance; there are no other meters.

Payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record. We never see, handle, or store your card details.
