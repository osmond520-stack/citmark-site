Frequently asked questions

Is the snapshot really free?

Yes. One snapshot is free — no card, no account, no charge when it ends. We email the report to the address you give us. The check is rate-limited to one snapshot per email per day to keep it fair.

Is this a free plan or a free trial?

Neither. A free plan would be an ongoing account with limits; a trial would be full access that expires. This is a single free check: we run 3 questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini once, and send you the results. Nothing to cancel, nothing that converts to a paid plan on its own.

What exactly do I get?

A per-question breakdown: for each of the 3 questions and each of the 3 engines, whether your brand was mentioned, which competitors were named in the same answers, and your visibility score for the run — the share of answers that mentioned your brand, computed from the answers actually collected.

Why only 3 questions?

Because it's a snapshot, not a full measurement. Three questions across three engines is nine real answers — enough to show you where you stand and how the full check works, with the honest caveat that a sample that size carries a wide confidence interval. Paid plans run larger question sets that you re-run on demand over time.

What does it cost to keep tracking after the free check?

Starter is $25/mo during launch pricing ($35/mo standard) — one brand, with a monthly points balance that every check draws from. It is priced for a small team to run real measurement without an enterprise budget; larger sets and more brands sit in the higher plans on the pricing page.
