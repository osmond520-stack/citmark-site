— What “free” means here

A snapshot, not a trial

To be precise about what “free” means here: this is a free snapshot, not a free plan and not a free trial. There is no time-limited account and nothing converts or starts charging when it ends.

You get one real check, measured from live answers — and if you want to keep checking over time, the paid plans are priced for small teams rather than enterprise budgets.

— Questions

Straight answers

Is the snapshot really free?

Yes. One snapshot is free — no card, no account, no charge when it ends. We email the report to the address you give us. The check is rate-limited to one snapshot per email per day to keep it fair.

Is this a free plan or a free trial?

Neither. A free plan would be an ongoing account with limits; a trial would be full access that expires. This is a single free check: we run 3 questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini once, and send you the results.

What do I get in the report?

Your visibility score, the answer to each question, whether your brand was mentioned, and which competitors were named instead — question by question, engine by engine.
