Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

1. Who we are

CitMark ("we", "us") operates citmark.com. For any privacy question or request: support@citmark.com.

2. What we collect

The only personal data we collect on this page is your email address, which you provide when joining the waitlist. We do not use cookies, analytics, fingerprinting, or any other tracking technology on this page.

3. Why we collect it, and the lawful basis

  • Purpose: to send you one notification when CitMark launches (including the founding-member pricing you signed up for), and to administer the waitlist (e.g., prevent duplicate signups).
  • Lawful basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): your consent, given when you submit the waitlist form after reading the notice next to it.

4. How we store and protect it

  • Your email is stored in a private, access-restricted Google Sheet and in our mailbox (signup notifications only).
  • We never sell your data. We share it with no third party except the service providers strictly necessary to run this page (static web hosting, email delivery), each bound by their own data-protection terms.

5. International transfers

Our team and some providers operate outside your country (including the United States and China). Where the GDPR applies, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, including the Standard Contractual Clauses with our providers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist emails until 12 months after launch, or until you ask us to delete them — whichever comes first. Deleted on request within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You may at any time: access, correct, or delete your data; withdraw your consent; restrict or object to processing; request portability; and (in the EU/UK) lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Email support@citmark.com for any of these — we respond within 30 days. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out.

8. Children

This page is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will post any updates here with a new effective date. Material changes will be notified to the waitlist by email.
