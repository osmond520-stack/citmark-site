How it works

The short version: we ask AI assistants the questions your buyers actually ask, and record whether your brand gets mentioned in the answers. Measured, not modeled.

  • Organic visibility only — whether AI assistants name your brand on their own in answers to real buying questions. Paid placements are never counted in your score.
  • Buyer-intent questions — “best X for Y” lists, comparisons, scenario advice. We deliberately don't pad scores with branded questions.
  • The engines — every full check runs across 4 AI engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude. Growth adds Grok, Pro adds DeepSeek, Teams adds Mistral.
  • The rhythm — a full measurement runs weekly; between full runs, we watch your core questions daily and alert you to sudden moves.
  • What counts — visibility score = answers mentioning your brand ÷ valid answers × 100. Failed or timed-out runs don't count toward the score — they're reported separately as an error rate.
  • What you get — every week: your score change, what drove it, and 3 concrete actions — on-site, off-site, and technical.

We don't guarantee AI recommendations or rankings — no honest tool can. We measure credibly, explain plainly, and help you improve.

FAQ

How do the checks actually work?

Each week we send your question set to each engine and capture the answers. We extract brand mentions and the sources cited, aggregate citations by site, and compute your visibility score. If an engine fails or times out, that run is excluded from the score and flagged as an error — we never silently fill gaps.

Which AI engines are covered?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude on every plan. Growth adds Grok, Pro adds DeepSeek, Teams adds Mistral. Every engine runs the same questions, so scores stay comparable across engines and over time.

Do I need to install anything or share analytics access?

No. There's nothing to install, and we don't need access to your analytics. If you later choose to connect Google Search Console, that's a separate, explicit authorization — always your call.

I already have an SEO tool. Why do I need this?

SEO tools optimise for Google rankings — but AI answers don't show rankings, they cite sources. You can rank #1 and still never be mentioned when a buyer asks ChatGPT. CitMark monitors the thing SEO tools can't see: whether AI actually names and cites you. Same for Google Search Console: it shows how Google sees your site; CitMark shows how AI engines see it.

What if my first report finds nothing useful?

Then you shouldn't pay for it. If your first report finds nothing useful, we'll refund you — no questions asked. Just email us and it's done.

How is this different from Profound or AthenaHQ?

Three differences. Who it's built for: they're built for enterprise teams with sales-led contracts (around $299+/month); CitMark is self-serve — you sign up yourself, no sales calls. How it measures: measured, not modeled — real buyer questions sent to real AI engines, counting actual mentions, instead of black-box estimates. What you get: not just a score, but 3 actions every week — because a number without a next step is just anxiety.

Refunds

  • Within 7 days of purchase: full refund, no questions asked — except: one no-questions refund per domain and per payment method (a second signup with the same domain or card doesn't get another one), and if you've already run 2 or more full snapshots, the refund is prorated instead.

  • After 30 days: monthly plans — no refund for the current period; cancel anytime and keep access until the period ends. Annual plans — prorated refund for remaining whole months.
  • Usage already consumed (e.g., overage re-runs that already ran) is non-refundable.
  • Billing errors (duplicate or wrong charges) are always fixed: tell us and we'll sort it out within 24 hours.
  • Founding-member spots don't go back into the pool after a refund. Accounts terminated for fraud or abuse aren't eligible for refunds.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method. We initiate within 3 business days; banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.

Privacy & data

  • Your data is yours. Workspaces are strictly isolated. We never sell data. Every employee access is audited. Cancel means delete.
  • Where it lives: your data lives in Singapore (primary database). The website itself is served globally. Email delivery (Resend) and payment processing (Creem) run in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • What we never collect: payment card details. Payments go through Creem, our merchant of record — card data never touches our systems.
  • Export: download all your personal data as JSON from your account settings, anytime.
  • Delete: delete your account and we remove your personal data from our live systems within 30 days. Encrypted backups rotate out within ~90 days and are never used for anything else.
