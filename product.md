# Measure. Diagnose. Draft.

CitMark measures how AI engines answer about your brand, diagnoses what's keeping you out, and drafts the corrections — from your confirmed facts, straight into your approval inbox.

Create your free account: https://citmark.com/signup/

## One loop, three steps

### Measure — A score you can re-check

The same buyer questions, put to every engine on your plan, with every answer recorded. Run it again and the change is measured, not guessed.

- Visibility score with a 95% Wilson band
- Mention rate per engine
- Run-over-run change

Sample: Visibility score 22.2 — Good Molecules — 95% CI 6.3–54.7 — Sample run Nov 13, 2026

### Diagnose — What's keeping you out — and why

Every answer is read for who got cited instead of you, what's stated about you that's wrong, and whether AI can read your pages at all.

- Citation gaps: sources citing rivals, not you
- Wrong facts, found and classified
- Readiness issues on your key pages

### Draft — Corrections drafted. Nothing sent.

For each wrong fact, CitMark drafts the correction and files it in your approval inbox. Written from your confirmed facts — and Nothing is sent from here.

- Correction drafts in your approval inbox
- Your facts — the only source a draft may use

Drafts wait in your approval inbox. You review, you send — CitMark never publishes or contacts a source for you.

## Measured, not modeled

### How the score works

Your score is the share of valid answers that mention your brand, shown with a 95% Wilson band at the run's real sample size. Answers that failed don't count, and a source is only ever shown when an engine actually returned it.

Full method: https://citmark.com/methodology/

### The facts a draft may use

Drafts are written from your facts — the statements you confirm in Your facts, and nothing else. That's why a correction drafted by CitMark never invents a claim about your business.

### Do you send or publish corrections for me?

No. Drafts wait in your approval inbox — you review them, you send them. CitMark never publishes a correction or contacts a source for you.

Just your domain. No code, no installation — your first snapshot runs on your questions right away.
