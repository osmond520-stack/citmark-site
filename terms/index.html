1. What CitMark does

CitMark measures how often AI assistants mention your brand in their answers — measured, not modeled. We explain what we find and suggest what to do next. In short: we credibly measure, explain, and improve your visibility in AI answers.

2. Your account

You need an account to use CitMark. Keep your login credentials safe — you're responsible for activity under your account. Plans include the number of seats shown on the pricing page; don't share logins beyond that.

3. Plans and billing

Current plans and prices are on our pricing page — that's the source of truth. Subscriptions renew automatically each billing period until you cancel. You can cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the current period.

Payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record. We never see, handle, or store your card details.

Founding members: if you joined under the founding offer (50% off for 12 months, first 50 members), your discount applies for 12 months from signup, then renews at the standard price. We'll email you 30 days before the change.

4. Refunds

  • Within 7 days of purchase: full refund, no questions asked — except: one no-questions refund per domain and per payment method (a second signup with the same domain or card doesn't get another one), and if you've already run 2 or more full snapshots, the refund is prorated instead.
  • Days 8–30: prorated refund for the unused period. Annual plans: refund for remaining whole months, with used months billed at the monthly rate.
  • After 30 days: monthly plans — no refund for the current period; cancel anytime and keep access until the period ends. Annual plans — prorated refund for remaining whole months.
  • Usage already consumed (e.g., overage re-runs that already ran) is non-refundable — the cost has already been incurred.
  • Billing errors (duplicate or wrong charges) are always fixed: tell us and we'll sort it out within 24 hours.
  • Founding-member spots don't go back into the pool after a refund.
  • Accounts terminated for fraud or abuse aren't eligible for refunds.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method. We initiate within 3 business days; banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.

Don't abuse the service: no reselling measurements, no scraping, no gaming the scores, no creating duplicate accounts to claim multiple refunds or evade limits. We may suspend or terminate accounts that do.

How we collect, use, and protect your data is described in our Privacy Policy. The short version: your data is yours — we never sell it, workspaces are strictly isolated, and deleting your account deletes your data from our live systems within 30 days.
