About CitMark

Why we exist

Buying changed. People ask AI instead of searching — "what's the best X for my situation?" — and AI answers from what it knows. If it never mentions your brand, you don't exist in that moment. Small businesses had no way to see this. We built CitMark to fix that.

What we do

We credibly measure, explain, and improve your visibility in AI answers. Every week you get your score, what moved it, and 3 actions to take. That's the whole product: no enterprise sales calls, no 40-tab dashboards.

Who it's for

Overseas small businesses — cross-border sellers, Shopify stores, independent brands selling internationally. The ones enterprise tools priced out. Self-serve, a clear price, useful from week one.

Our mission

Make AI visibility measurable — and improvable — for every small business, not just enterprises.

FAQ

How is this different from Profound or AthenaHQ?

Three differences. Who it's built for: they're built for enterprise teams with sales-led contracts (around $299+/month); CitMark is self-serve — you sign up yourself, no sales calls. How it measures: measured, not modeled — real buyer questions sent to real AI engines, counting actual mentions, instead of black-box estimates. What you get: not just a score, but 3 actions every week — because a number without a next step is just anxiety.
