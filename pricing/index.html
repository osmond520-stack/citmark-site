— FAQ

Straight answers.

What happens when the 12-month founding price ends?

Your plan renews at the standard price — then $35/$70/$169/mo.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. Please contact our support team and we’ll sort it out with you.

What if I cancel?

You keep access until the end of the current billing period, and you won’t be charged again. Your data is handled per our Privacy Policy.

+

Do you charge per seat or per check?

No. One plan covers your sites — you don’t pay per seat, and checks draw from your plan, not a per-check meter.
