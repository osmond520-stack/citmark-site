— Method note
1 · Question set

50 constructed buyer questions in this category (44 non-price, 6 price-intent), written for this run; no question names a brand being measured. This page reports the 44 non-price questions; price-intent questions are reported in their own layer on the leaderboard.

2 · Engines

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, run in grounded (live-search) form on 2026-11-24: 150 of 150 question–engine cells completed. Engines not run are not listed as absent — they were not measured.

3 · Run form

Run form: grounded — every engine answered with live web search on. Rates are micro-averaged and always shown with their n and 95% Wilson interval: with n = 44 per engine, a gap of one or two answers sits inside the interval and is not a ranking.

4 · Small samples

Brands or cells with n < 30 are marked “insufficient sample” and shown as raw counts only — no ranking judgement.
